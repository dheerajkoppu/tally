# Code of Conduct

Tally follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/dheerajkoppu). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may block people who don't follow this code.
